How it works

  1. Tell us about your nights

    About 25 quick questions about your nights, your body, your days and your evenings. Three minutes.

  2. Get your likely suspects

    Your Sleep Score and the three things most likely to be waking you, ranked.

  3. Test one change for 7 nights

    For example: no wine after 7 PM. Each evening, a 3-minute wind-down. Each morning, a 30-second check-in.

  4. See what actually works for you

    On day 7 you get a clear answer, like “2 fewer wake-ups on nights without wine.” Then the next test.

Questions

Is this medical treatment?

No. SleepThrough is a habit coach, not a diagnosis or a replacement for your doctor. If you snore loudly, stop breathing at night, or feel very low, talk to a doctor. Ask them about hormone therapy too — SleepThrough works alongside it.

Do I have to quit wine?

No. You test one change for 7 nights and see what your sleep does. Some women find wine is their trigger. Many find it isn't.

Do I need a sleep tracker?

No. A 30-second morning check-in is enough. A tracker adds detail if you have one.

Where is my data?

In this preview, everything you enter stays on your device. We never sell health data.
